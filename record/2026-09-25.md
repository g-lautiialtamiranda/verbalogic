# 2026-09-25

## Built
- First working version: select text anywhere, press Ctrl+Alt+D, and a popup shows the translation in two languages (+PT), synonyms, example sentences, every meaning of a word with arrows, and optional AI rewrites (OpenRouter).
- Moved the dictionary to Google's `dict-chrome-ex` endpoint after `gtx` got blocked.
- **F-01** Section titles: turned off kerning and used whole-pixel letter-spacing on the small capitals ("OT" was colliding).
- **F-02** OpenRouter hint: shown only for the first 3 lookups, as a "Set up rewrites" button that opens Settings.
- **F-03** Meaning list: click "Meaning 2 of 8 ▾" or press Alt+↓ to jump to any meaning.
- **F-04** Empty states: a meaning with no synonyms hides the section.

## Decided against
- **U-01** Cleaning punctuation before lookups. Punctuation can change the meaning, so text is sent as selected.
