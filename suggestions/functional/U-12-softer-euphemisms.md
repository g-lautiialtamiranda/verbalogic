# U-12 · Softer ways to say it (euphemisms and tactful phrasing)

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** S

## What
A "softer" tone in "Other ways to say it", next to casual / neutral / formal: the euphemism or the polite, diplomatic way people say something delicate.

| You select | Softer in Spanish | Softer in English |
|---|---|---|
| *lo despidieron* | **lo desvincularon** · **prescindieron de sus servicios** | **he was let go** · **his position was eliminated** |
| *murió* | **falleció** · **nos dejó** · **pasó a mejor vida** | **passed away** · **is no longer with us** |
| *you're wrong* | **no sé si lo veo así** · **capaz hay otra forma de verlo** | **I see it a bit differently** · **I'm not sure that's quite right** |
| *es caro* | **no es de lo más económico** | **it's on the pricey side** |

It uses the same AI request as today's rewrites (one more tone in the prompt), and only shows up when a softer version exists.

## Why
Delicate topics come up in work emails and messages all the time: bad news, disagreement, money, health. Knowing the euphemism people actually use is what makes a message sound natural and polite instead of blunt or translated.
