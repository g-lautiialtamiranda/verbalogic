# U-02 · Spanish synonyms when Google has none

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** S

## What
Spanish has no free equivalent of Datamuse. But Google already sends the Spanish alternatives of the English word (for "hello": ¡Hola!, ¡Oiga!, ¡Diga!, ¡Aló!…). When the Spanish column has no synonyms, show those as "Similar words". No extra request needed.

## Why
The Spanish column is often empty where the English one has synonyms.
