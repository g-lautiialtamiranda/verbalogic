# U-10 · Compact bubble for single words

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** M

## What
For a single word, first show a small card next to the mouse (headword, main translation, speaker, star), like the macOS Look Up bubble. Press the shortcut again, or click "More", to expand it into today's full popup. Longer text keeps opening the full popup.

## Why
Most lookups are one word where you only need the translation. A small bubble interrupts less than a large window in the middle of the screen.

## References
- [Lookupper for macOS](https://lookupper.com/mac/): point, press a hotkey, get a small popup
- [Easydict](https://github.com/tisfeng/Easydict): a compact, elegant lookup window
