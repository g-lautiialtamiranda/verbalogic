# U-13 · Real everyday sentences (free, no key)

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** S

## What
Show a few short, real sentences that use the word or phrase, each with its translation, taken from **Tatoeba**: a free, open collection of everyday sentences written and translated by people. The API needs no key. A quick test with *aprovechar* found 27 sentences, such as *"Deberías aprovechar esta oportunidad."* → *"You should take advantage of this chance."*

- Goes under "In real use" (or as a "More examples" link), cached like the rest.
- Pairs with the meaning switcher: if a sentence contains the selected meaning's translation, it goes first.
- Credit Tatoeba (CC BY) in the README.

## Why
Google's examples are often literary or news-like. Tatoeba's are the simple, daily-life sentences you'd actually say, in both languages at once. It's also the free fallback for U-11 when there's no AI key.
