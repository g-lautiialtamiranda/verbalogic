# U-09 · Formal / informal labels on synonyms

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** S

## What
Google marks some synonyms with a register (formal, informal, colloquial, dated, rare…) in `label_info`. VerbaLogic already reads it, but only to put those words last, and then drops the label. Keep it and show it: a small muted tag on the chip ("ameliorate · formal"), or a lighter chip style for informal words.

## Why
When you're writing an email you want to know which synonym is the formal one. It's data we already download.
