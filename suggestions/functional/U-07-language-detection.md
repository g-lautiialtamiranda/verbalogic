# U-07 · Better language detection for ambiguous words

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** S

## What
Google detected "banco" typed alone as English. When the detected language isn't one of yours, or the text comes back unchanged in the other language, ask again with Spanish or English set as the source language and keep whichever gives a real translation.

## Why
Short words that exist in several languages end up in the wrong columns today.
