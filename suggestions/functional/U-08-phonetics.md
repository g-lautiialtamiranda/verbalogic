# U-08 · Phonetic transcription for English words

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** S

## What
Show the IPA under English headwords (improve → /ɪmˈpruːv/), next to the speaker button. The free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev, no key) returns it. It didn't answer in time during a quick test, so it needs a short timeout, a cache, and nothing on screen when it fails. Spanish is written as it sounds, so it doesn't need this.

## Why
It pairs with the pronunciation button: you hear the word *and* see how it's built. It's also part of what a formal dictionary entry looks like (F-05).
