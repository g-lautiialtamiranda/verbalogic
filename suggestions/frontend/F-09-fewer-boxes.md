# F-09 · Fewer boxes, more whitespace

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** M

## What
A pass over the whole popup's layout: an 8-px spacing grid, one set of corner radii (for example 16 card / 10 inner / 8 chips), and columns separated by a thin divider and whitespace instead of each being a bordered box. Section titles can then be lighter too. Compare before and after with screenshots.

## Why
Today there's a box (the card) holding boxes (columns) holding boxes (chips, rewrites). Removing a layer is what most elegant reading interfaces do: the content becomes the design.
