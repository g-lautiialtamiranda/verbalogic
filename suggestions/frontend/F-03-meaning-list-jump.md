# F-03 · Click "Meaning 2 of 8" to see all meanings

**Status:** done (2026-09-25)  
**Effort:** S

## What
Make the meaning label clickable. It opens a small list with each meaning's part of speech and the start of its definition ("1 noun · land beside a river", "5 noun · financial institution"…). Click one to jump to it. The arrows stay for quick stepping.

## Why
With 8 meanings, pressing › seven times to reach the one you want is slow. The list also shows at a glance whether your meaning is there.

## What changed
Click "Meaning 2 of 8 ▾", or press **Alt+↓**, to get a list of every meaning (number, part of speech, start of the definition). Pick one to jump to it. The popup stays open while the list is showing.
