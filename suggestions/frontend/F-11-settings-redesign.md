# F-11 · Settings like Windows 11 Settings

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** M

## What
Replace the tabs with a left sidebar (General, Languages, Appearance, Rewrites, History, About), grouped rows with short descriptions, and a live preview of the popup in Appearance while you change theme, accent (F-07) and font size. Add a History page (clear recent, turn history off, how many to keep) and an About page (version, links).

## Why
Settings is the second screen people see. Matching the Windows 11 layout feels native and formal, and the preview removes the change-save-look loop.
