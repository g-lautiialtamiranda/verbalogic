# F-08 · Calm motion and loading states

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** S

## What
- A short fade and small upward slide (about 120 ms) when the popup opens.
- Grey placeholder bars (skeletons) while translations and synonyms load, instead of "…" and "Looking up synonyms…".
- A soft cross-fade when switching meanings, so it's clear what changed.

All of it off when Windows has "Animation effects" turned off.

## Why
The popup already appears fast. Small, quiet motion makes it feel polished instead of abrupt, and skeletons stop the layout from jumping while results arrive.
