# F-07 · A more formal palette

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** S

## What
Swap the bright purple default accent for a quieter one, and offer a few presets in Settings with a live preview: **ink blue**, **deep teal**, **oxblood**, **graphite**. Add an optional warm "paper" light theme (off-white background, dark ink text) next to the current light and dark themes. Use the accent more sparingly: selection, the active meaning and focus, not every chip hover.

## Why
Colour carries most of an app's tone. Muted ink colours read as serious and trustworthy, which suits a dictionary.
