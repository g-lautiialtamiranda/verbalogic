# F-04 · Quieter empty states

**Status:** done (2026-09-25)  
**Effort:** S

## What
When a meaning has no synonyms, the column shows a "SYNONYMS / No synonyms for this meaning." block. Hide the section instead, or show one muted line, so the useful parts move up.

## Why
Less noise, especially in the Spanish column, where synonyms are often missing.

## What changed
When a meaning has no synonyms, the section is hidden. "Looking up synonyms…" and Google-limit notes still show.
