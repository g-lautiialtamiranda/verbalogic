# I-01 · Pick the meaning from the sentence

**Status:** proposed  
**Effort:** L

## What
When you select a whole sentence and then double-click one of its words in the text box, send the sentence and the word to the AI (requires a key). The popup then opens directly on the right meaning ("Meaning 5 of 8 · financial").

## Why
The closest thing to the context-aware meanings you described as hard, and it only runs when you ask for it.
